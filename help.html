How to use the on-call rota

← Back to the rota

1. Put it on your phone's home screen

Then it opens like an app, with one tap.

1

Open the rota link in Safari (it must be Safari, not Chrome or the WhatsApp browser). If WhatsApp opened it for you, tap the ⋯ or share icon and choose Open in Safari.

2

Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).

3

Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.

4

Tap Add in the top right. A blue UHCW Rota icon appears on your home screen.

1

Open the rota link in Chrome. If WhatsApp opened it in its own window, tap the ⋮ menu and choose Open in Chrome.

2

Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right of Chrome.

3

Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install app).

4

Tap Add. A blue UHCW Rota icon appears on your home screen.

2. Sign in (first time only)

1

Open the rota. Tap Sign in at the top right.

2

Type your UHCW email (firstname.surname@uhcw.nhs.uk) and the password ROTA. Tap Continue.

3

Choose your team. Your name is filled in from your email, so you can't pick the wrong person. Tap Create my account.

You stay signed in on that phone. The first time you change something each day, the app asks for the password again, so nothing changes by accident.

3. Read the calendar

  • Red days are your team's on-call days: every Wednesday and your team weekend (Friday to Sunday) every 4 weeks.
  • OC = on call. L = long weekend. S = short weekend. FC = who is in fracture clinic. PT = post-take Monday.
  • Your own name has a blue outline so you can spot it quickly.
  • Tap any day to see the full names and details.
  • Other teams show as small grey tags, for information.

4. Swap an on-call

1

Tap a red day where you are on call.

2

Tap Ask someone to cover and choose the colleague.

3

If you will do one of their on-calls in return, pick it from the list. Otherwise leave it as "Nothing in return".

4

Tap Send request. Nothing changes on the rota until your colleague says yes.

You can also offer to cover someone else: tap their red day and tap Offer to cover.

5. Answer a request

1

When someone asks you, a red number appears on Notifications at the top, and a yellow box appears at the top of the page.

2

Open it and tap Approve or Decline. Type your password if asked.

3

If you approve, the rota updates by itself for everyone.

6. Fracture clinic

On a clinic day the app assumes you are in clinic unless you are on call or away. If that is wrong, tap the day and tap In clinic or Not in next to your own name. Tap Reset to go back to the automatic setting. The day shows red if nobody is covering the clinic.

7. Leave

1

Tap a red day (any on-call day), scroll to Away / leave.

2

Set the from and to dates, the type (annual, study, sick) and tap Add.

3

If your leave covers one of your on-calls, the app tells you, so you can arrange a swap.

8. Change your regular week for good

Open Notifications, find My regular on-call week, choose the week and tap Request. Mr Kozdryk approves it and the whole rota moves.

9. Put your on-calls in your phone calendar

Tap Calendar file, choose your name, tap Download .ics file, then open the file and add it to Google Calendar or Outlook. Download it again after a swap; the calendar does not update by itself.

Questions, a wrong name, or a locked account: ask Mr Kozdryk.
